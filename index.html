The method

What is forest bathing?

Forest bathing is the English name for Shinrin Yoku, the Japanese practice of slowing down in the forest with all your senses. It was developed in Japan in the 1980s for stress management and has been studied ever since.

It is not a hike, not a training, and not meditation in nature. There is no destination and nothing to get right. A certified guide leads the whole experience.

What you will actually do

  • Meet at the agreed point. Short introduction.
  • Stroll under 500 meters into the forest.
  • The guide offers simple invitations: notice what is moving, sit with a tree, take in the forest with each sense.
  • Short sharing rounds between invitations.
  • A closing gathering, and back at the agreed time.

What to expect

  • 90 minutes to 3 hours, depending on the format.
  • Very little walking. Dress in layers; it is cooler in the shade of the forest. Held rain or shine.
  • You will stand, sit and be with nature. This is not fitness or exercise.
  • Participants say they are positively surprised to leave deeply relaxed.
  • The experience is for personal use, not professional use. We do not train trainers.

Why it works

Stress hormone

−13%

less cortisol in the forest than in the city, measured in 280 people across 24 forests

Park et al., 2010

Blood pressure

−6 points

off the top blood pressure reading, in people who arrived with high blood pressure

Ideno et al., 2017

Recovery

+56%

more rest-and-recover activity in the body, forest compared with a city street

Park et al., 2010

Brain

60 min

one walk in nature was enough to lower activity in the brain’s stress centre. A walk in the city was not

Sudimac et al., 2022

Across 25 trials and 1,876 participants, forest therapy produced a medium-to-large reduction in psychological stress (Qin et al., 2026).

FAQ

Is this team building?

Yes, and it works differently: no competition, nobody performs. Teams become curious, more connected and it strengthens team communication.

I am not outdoorsy. Can I come?

This is not exercise and we stroll under 500 meters to the outdoor location.

Can I come alone to a public event?

Most people do. The group is small and the format makes it easy to connect, without small talk.

What languages do you work in?

English, German and Italian, across Switzerland.

What does it cost?

Public event pricing is on the Nature Reset Club page. Private and team events are quoted after a short call or email.

Do you have events or travel to the French-speaking part of Switzerland?

For locations beyond the Zug, Zurich and Lucerne area, travel costs are added to the quote. It is recommended to come to Zug or Zurich to experience an event. We do not have a partner in the French-speaking area of Switzerland.

Do you train trainers, or train facilitators to become coaches or guides?

No. Our events are for personal use, not professional development. If you are a facilitator, coach or L&D professional, you are welcome as a participant. Our formats, sequences and materials are our work and are not available for reuse, licence or training. Contact ANFT to be trained as a guide.
